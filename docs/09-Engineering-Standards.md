**Document:** `09-Engineering-Standards.md`  
**Authority:** Subordinate to Master Vision → Founder Brief → Constitution

## Purpose

Establish engineering standards that keep the codebase scalable, maintainable, modular, and readable — while ensuring every change can be traced to constitutional principles and success metrics.

## Audience

All contributors writing code, reviewing pull requests, or evolving package boundaries.

## Scope

In scope:

- Repository and package conventions
- Documentation and decision-record expectations
- Naming and modularity rules (including brand-isolation guidance)
- Testing, review, and quality bars
- What not to do (placeholder code, premature frameworks, silent architectural decisions)

Out of scope (for now):

- Concrete lint/format toolchains
- CI workflow definitions
- Language/runtime version pins
