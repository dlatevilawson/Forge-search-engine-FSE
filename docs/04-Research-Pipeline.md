## Report Structure

Every research report must follow this Master Vision–defined structure:

1. Executive Summary
2. Research Objective
3. Key Findings
4. Evidence
5. Expert Consensus
6. Conflicting Evidence
7. Confidence Assessment
8. Important Unknowns
9. Recommended Next Steps
10. References
