## Purpose

Define how research artifacts, evidence, knowledge, workspaces, and long-term memory are persisted, versioned, and retrieved — in service of Accuracy, Transparency, Trust, Understanding, and Decision Quality.

## Audience

Backend engineers, data modelers, and anyone implementing storage for research sessions, evidence graphs, and project workspaces.

## Scope

In scope:

- Logical data domains (sessions, evidence, reports, knowledge, workspaces)
- Retention and update semantics for evolving knowledge
- Provenance and auditability requirements
- Boundaries between operational data and knowledge memory

Out of scope (for now):

- Concrete vendor selection (e.g. Supabase) and migrations
- Physical schemas and indexes
- Operational runbooks
